SECTION 6 — Smart Money Concepts (SMC) Engine
Ye hamare bot ka sab se important analysis module hoga.
Technical Indicators market ko mathematically dekhte hain, jab ke SMC market ko institutional nazariye se analyze karega.
Ye module hamare bot ki core strength hoga.

•	Institutional movement identify karna.

•	AI Engine ko high-quality context provide karna.
