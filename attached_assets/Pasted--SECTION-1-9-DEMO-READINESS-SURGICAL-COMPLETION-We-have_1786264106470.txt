## Pipeline behavior

When a new completed candle triggers the scanner:

1. Scan the relevant pair/timeframe.
2. Ignore `None` / invalid opportunities.
3. Pass valid candidate data into the existing AI Engine.
4. If AI does not approve / confidence is insufficient:

   * stop processing
   * do NOT call Risk Manager for execution
   * do NOT execute a trade.
5. If AI approves:

   * obtain the AI direction/entry/SL/TP data required by the existing architecture.
6. Pass the proposed trade into Risk Manager.
7. Risk Manager performs the complete existing Section 8 approval.
8. If Risk Manager rejects:

   * do NOT call Trade Manager broker execution.
   * log the rejection reason.
9. If Risk Manager approves:

   * pass the immutable `RiskApproval` into Trade Manager.
10. Trade Manager executes through the existing MT5 connector only.
