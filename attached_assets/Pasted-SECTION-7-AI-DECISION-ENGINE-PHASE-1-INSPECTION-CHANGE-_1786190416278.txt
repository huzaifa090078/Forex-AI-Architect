DECISIONS

The engine must produce exactly one of:

- BUY
- SELL
- NO_TRADE

Do not introduce additional decision states unless the existing architecture requires one.

CONFIDENCE

Every decision must contain a confidence score.

Version 1 minimum confidence threshold:
- Use 80% as the default threshold unless the existing project already defines a different configuration pattern.
- Do not hard-code the threshold if the project has an appropriate configuration/settings architecture.
- Confidence below the threshold must result in NO_TRADE.

CONFLUENCE

The engine must not make a decision from one indicator alone.

The decision should consider agreement between:

- trend
- SMC
- technical indicators
- spread
- session
- volatility
- market conditions

TRADE QUALITY

The engine should classify setup quality as:

- Excellent
- Good
- Average
- Weak
- Reject

Only sufficiently strong setups should be forwarded as actionable decisions.

ENTRY

The engine may produce:

- current entry price
- suggested entry zone when waiting for a better entry is preferable

STOP LOSS

The engine may produce a suggested SL candidate/zone.

This is NOT the final SL.

Risk Manager will validate/finalize it later.

TAKE PROFIT

The engine may produce suggested TP candidate levels.

These are NOT final TP levels.

Risk Manager will validate them later.

REJECTION CONDITIONS

The engine should reject a setup when applicable:

- trend is unclear
- SMC conflicts with direction
- technical indicators materially conflict
- spread is too high
- volatility is unsuitable
- confidence is below threshold

IMPORTANT:
Do not invent arbitrary numerical spread/volatility/session thresholds during implementation.
First inspect existing configuration and risk-related architecture.
If a required threshold does not exist, identify it as a design decision in the Change Plan instead of silently inventing it.

CONTINUOUS MONITORING

The architecture should allow a future/current signal to be recalculated when market conditions change.

For an unexecuted signal, the decision may change from BUY/SELL to NO_TRADE when the setup becomes invalid.

Do not implement trade cancellation or order modification because execution is out of scope.

CRITICAL DESIGN RULE

Do NOT create an artificial "AI" that simply calculates a random or arbitrary confidence number.

Confidence must be derived deterministically from the actual available confluence inputs.

Also do not claim that a rule-based engine is machine-learning AI.
Call it the AI Decision Engine because that is the system module defined by the project, but implement Version 1 as deterministic rule-based confluence logic.

SAFETY / EXECUTION BOUNDARY

The AI Engine must NOT:

- send orders
- modify orders
- close positions
- calculate actual position size
- bypass Risk Manager

Technical Indicators
    ↓

AI Decision Engine
    ↓

    ↓
[future execution layer]
