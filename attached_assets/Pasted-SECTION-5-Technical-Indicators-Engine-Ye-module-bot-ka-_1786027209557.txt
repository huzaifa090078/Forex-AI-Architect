Is ka kaam sirf indicators calculate karna hai.
Ye:

•	❌ Buy/Sell signal nahi banayega. 
•	❌ Risk calculate nahi karega. 
Sirf calculations karega aur result dusre modules ko dega.

5.3 Indicators
1. EMA (Exponential Moving Average)
Purpose
Trend identify karna.
EMA Values
•	EMA 20 
•	EMA 50 
•	EMA 100 
•	EMA 200 
Use
•	Trend direction 
•	Dynamic support/resistance 
•	Trend confirmation 
________________________________________
2. SMA (Simple Moving Average)
Purpose
Long-term trend dekhna.
SMA Values
•	SMA 20 
•	SMA 50 
•	SMA 100 
•	SMA 200 
Use
•	Trend confirmation 
•	Market direction 
________________________________________
3. RSI (Relative Strength Index)
Purpose
Overbought aur Oversold market identify karna.
Range
•	0 – 100 
Important Levels
•	Above 70 → Overbought 
•	Below 30 → Oversold 
•	Around 50 → Neutral 
Use
•	Momentum 
•	Reversal confirmation 
________________________________________
4. MACD
Purpose
Momentum aur trend strength.
Components
•	MACD Line 
•	Signal Line 
•	Histogram 
Use
•	Momentum change 
•	Trend confirmation 
•	Crossovers 
________________________________________
5. ATR (Average True Range)
Purpose
Market volatility measure karna.
Use
•	SL calculation 
•	TP calculation 
•	Market volatility 
________________________________________
6. Bollinger Bands
Components
•	Upper Band 
•	Middle Band 
•	Lower Band 
Use
•	Volatility 
•	Range detection 
•	Breakout detection 
________________________________________
7. VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)
Purpose
Institutional average price.
Use
•	Trend bias 
•	Fair value 
•	Institutional confirmation 
________________________________________
8. ADX (Average Directional Index)
Purpose
Trend ki strength measure karna.
Components
•	ADX 
•	+DI 
•	-DI 
Levels
•	Below 20 → Weak Trend 
•	20–25 → Developing Trend 
•	Above 25 → Strong Trend 
________________________________________
9. Stochastic RSI
Purpose
Fast momentum indicator.
Components
•	%K 
•	%D 
Use
•	Early reversal 
•	Momentum confirmation 
________________________________________
10. Volume Analysis
Engine Check Karega
•	Current Volume 
•	Average Volume 
•	Volume Spike 
•	Volume Drop 
•	OBV (On Balance Volume) 
Use
•	Breakout confirmation 
•	Institutional activity
